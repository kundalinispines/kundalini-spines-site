About

About Kundalini Spines

Kundalini Spines is a two-member underground hip-hop project built from sacred geometry, coded lyricism, street experience, and the architecture of the spine itself. Nothing here is meant to be fully explained — only received, and decoded at whatever pace each listener is ready for.

The Messengers

Two Messengers. One Signal.

The Messengers don’t claim mastery over the Signal. They receive it, preserve it, and carry it forward without weakening its meaning — witnesses first, performers second.

The Seeker

Receives what others miss. Reads the pattern before the sentence.

The Alchemist

Turns pressure into record. Carries what the Signal leaves behind.

Creative Philosophy

Sacred and Street

The project moves between the sacred and the street — Metatron’s Cube next to a blueprint, an X-ray beside a manuscript. Ancient knowledge, modern static. The spine sits at the center of all of it: thirty-three vertebrae, seven nodes, one signal moving through the whole structure. None of it is decoration. Every symbol carries weight, or it doesn’t appear at all.

Transmissions

What a Transmission Is

Numbered pieces called Transmissions carry the Signal forward — visual, lyrical, sometimes both. Each one is filed once and never reused. Transmission 001 is the first.
